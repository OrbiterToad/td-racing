APEX / The Circuit Collection

Civilian traffic art: Kenney Racing Pack, by Kenney Vleugels.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-pack
Download: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-pack
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 / public domain).
Original license: assets/kenney/License.txt
Used files: civilian traffic car style 2
in red, blue, yellow and black, from PNG/Cars/. Legacy garage and tree sprites stay bundled. No asset service is required at runtime.

All six garage cars use original top-down SVG vehicle art created for
this game: Clubsport R, Sprint GT, Offroad, Drift Spec, Iron V8 and Formula X.
Local files: assets/cars/{clubsport,sprint,rally,drift,muscle,formula}-*.svg.
Every model has five paint colors. Flat colors, light trim and rounded
edges match the existing scenery and civilian car sprites.
The scenery pack in assets/scenery/*.svg is original vector art created
for this game: eleven tree species, homes/cabins/huts, offices, warehouses,
greenhouses, cafés, garden sheds, six themed garage modules, two control
rooms, team canopies, transporter trucks, grandstands, tyre and fuel racks,
water tanks, tool trolleys, crates, garden beds, mossy rocks and park furniture.
Regenerate these 48 models with python3 tools/build-scenery-assets.py.
The game raster-caches the pack once, then bakes static scenery into the
world canvas.
All scenery is drawn from directly overhead; there are no side-view assets.

Road, gravel, grass, curbs, tire marks, roof planes, and UI: original procedural art.
Desert Canyon sand, sandstone contour formations, boulders, and overhead
cactus crowns: original procedural art drawn in main.js.
Alpine snow, overhead pine crowns, mountain contours, ice patches, oil
slicks, sand drifts, selection previews, and garage UI: original procedural art.
