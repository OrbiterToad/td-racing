APEX / The Circuit Collection

Car and overhead tree art: Kenney Racing Pack, by Kenney Vleugels.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-pack
Download: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-pack
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 / public domain).
Original license: assets/kenney/License.txt
Used files: PNG/Objects/tree_large.png and car styles 1 (Clubsport R),
5 (Sprint GT), and 4 (Offroad) in red, blue, green, yellow, and black,
from PNG/Cars/. Sprites remain local; no asset service is required at runtime.
All scenery is drawn from directly overhead; there are no side-view assets.

Road, gravel, grass, curbs, tire marks, roof planes, and UI: original procedural art.
Desert Canyon sand, sandstone contour formations, boulders, and overhead
cactus crowns: original procedural art drawn in main.js.
Alpine snow, overhead pine crowns, mountain contours, ice patches, oil
slicks, sand drifts, selection previews, and garage UI: original procedural art.

p5.js 1.11.3, LGPL-2.1, https://p5js.org/
CDN: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/p5@1.11.3/lib/p5.min.js
Local fallback: assets/p5.min.js
